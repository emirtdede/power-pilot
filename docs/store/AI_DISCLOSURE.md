## EN - Store Description Disclosure

> Power Pilot includes an on-device AI assistant. Your voice and commands
> never leave your computer; all processing happens locally. The AI only
> proposes power-automation tasks and never shuts down or restarts your PC
> without your approval.

## Partner Center - Sertifikasyon Notu (EN)

> This submission includes live generative AI (Section 11.16): an on-device
> assistant (Qwen3-1.7B, Apache-2.0, runs locally via node-llama-cpp, no cloud
> calls) that converts natural-language input into power-automation task
> proposals. All executions go through the existing safety pipeline and user
> confirmation flow. In-app reporting: About > Report inappropriate content
> (mailto support@vellium.dev). Demo mode: AI works without an account; if no
> model is installed the assistant shows setup guidance instead of failing.
